0. Result
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The port (step S3: clock, day cycle, movement, camera) equals the original BIT FOR BIT, in all seven traces (6 standard + the long run), for every step before
the first effect the port does not have yet. The boundary is computed from the reference trace itself (header.boundaries, tools/lib/trace_io.mjs firstEffects;
godot/tests/test_reference_trace.gd computes it again and fails if the two disagree):
  contact  enemy/update draws gameRandom, or hp differs from step 1, or the damage flash is on (a hit; it also pushes the player 22 px)
  fire     player.attackAnim is not 0 (a weapon shot sets attackAnim, attackAngle and lastFacing; the player does not move)

Movement / camera / time / day-phase / hp fields are compared up to the step before the contact (compare_trace.mjs below, and the Godot test).
The animation fields attackAnim, attackAngle and lastFacing are compared on EVERY step before the contact boundary in which the reference shows no shot, teacher-forced
(the port's three values are set to the reference's values of the step before; the port cannot shoot): the countdown max(0, attackAnim - dt) and the rule
`if speed > 8: lastFacing = atan2(vy, vx)` are checked against many reference values (the first version compared 1 or 2 steps, where both sides are 0).
This comparison is in the Godot test (it needs the forcing, which compare_trace.mjs cannot express); its counts come from the full run in tests.txt.
