5. Note for S7
----------------------------------------------------------------------
signature_fx.webp is 512x2944. GLES 3.0 only guarantees GL_MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE >= 2048; real Android GLES3 devices report 4096 or more,
but this was not tested on a device (design 9.5). If S7 meets a device below 2944 it has to split the sheet in two (the layout is by
row, so a split at row 23 is straightforward).
