----------------------------------------------------------------------

[reported] Analyst B / implementer M, on their own input sets:
  * Math.round implemented as floor(x + 0.5) is wrong in 1.14 % of cases; the ceil-based Float64Round of V8 is what JsMath.js_round implements.
  * Godot builtin atan2 differs from V8 in 15.2 % of cases on desktop (matches the 15.55 % measured above); builtin sin/cos 0.06-0.07 % on
    desktop glibc with FMA and about 2.8 % on Bionic (Android).
  * Node 22's Math.sin/cos (fdlibm) differ from Chromium's (glibc algorithm) in about 3 % of cases, so the golden generator does not use Node's sin/cos;
    it uses tools/golden/_glibc_sin.mjs, a transcription of V8's s_sin.c with the branred path and the 440-entry table, verified against Chromium.
  * GDScript: float % float is a compile error; only fmod works (JsMath.js_mod). The design text still says otherwise.

Port scope (godot/sim/js_compat.gd, 1091 lines): sin, cos (glibc s_sin.c + branred), atan, atan2, exp, log (fdlibm), hypot (V8 FastMathHypot), js_round,
trunc, js_mod, js_max / js_min (NaN and -0 rules), max3 / min3 / max5 / min5 / max_n / min_n, clamp (= Math.max(lo, Math.min(hi, v))),
to_int32, to_uint32, imul, shl / sar / shr_u / i32, pow (wrapper: JS special cases, then the platform pow; NOT bit-guaranteed, unused by M1 systems).
Only operations that are exact on every platform are used inside: IEEE + - * /, comparisons, sqrt, floor, ceil, abs, fmod.

Reading: the ported transcendental functions cost about 0.4 to 1.2 us per call, 15 to 40 times a builtin (30 to 80 ns). The original makes 28 gameRandom
draws in frame 1 (29 in total after newGame's one draw; implementer G, from the original's own update()); the port makes none of them yet, they arrive with the

5. Seeded random numbers
----------------------------------------------------------------------
tests/golden/rng.json from the ORIGINAL makeSeededRandom (cut out by name, run in the empty vm sandbox): 8 seeds (424242, 24681357, 0, 1, 4294967295,
2147483648, 2654435769, 123456789) x 1000 outputs, compared as exact integers k with value = k / 2^32 (0 gives the stream of 2654435769, as in the original:
seed>>>0 || 2654435769); 17 seed normalisations (-1, -1.9, 1.9, +0, -0, 2^32, 2^32 + 1, 4294967295.5, 2^53, 1e20, -2^31, 2^31, 0.5, 2654435769, NaN, +-Infinity; given as IEEE bit patterns); 5 gameRandom helper cases (rand, randi, chance, shuffle,
the `gameRandom() * n | 0` idiom) with their draw counts. Chromium agrees: 8 streams x 1000 outputs + 17 normalisations, 0 mismatches (section 2).
GameRng wraps SeededRng and counts draws (gameRandomDraws); FxRng is a separate, seedable stream for effects and never touches the counter
(the original uses Math.random there).

6. Cost of the per-entity math with JsMath, reproduced for the review fixes  [measured now]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Scratch benchmark of the reviewer (scratchpad/s3/rv/bench_proj/bench.gd, a throwaway Godot project around an unchanged copy of godot/sim; it is not part of the repository),
re-run in this step on the same VM: typed kernels that replay the trig / exp / hypot calls of the original's per-entity code, once with JsMath and once with the engine builtins.
  per call          JsMath.sin 840 ns (builtin 36)   cos 828   atan2 682 (builtin 49)   hypot 321   exp 530
  100 enemies       moveBasicEnemy + steerEnemy + smoothEnemyVelocity (3 sin, 2 cos, 2 atan2, 2 exp, 5 hypot each):  JsMath 1126 us/step   builtin 219 us/step
  520 homing shots  updateProjectiles' homing branch (3 atan2, 2 sin, 2 cos, 1 hypot each):                          JsMath 3854 us/step   builtin 613 us/step
  520 orbitals      1 sin + 1 cos each:                                                                               JsMath 1238 us/step   builtin 293 us/step
  Sim.step (S3 systems, walking): median 39 us, p95 65-73 us
Reading: analyst B's estimate of about 0.4 ms for 100 enemies is about 3 times low and ignores projectiles; with JsMath the math of 100 enemies plus a few hundred homing or orbiting
projectiles alone uses 2.4-5.0 ms of the design 5.5 budget (Sim.step median <= 4 ms on desktop). Nothing was changed in JsMath for this: the kernels that decide the real cost (S5/S6) do
not exist yet, and a bit-exact speed-up should be shaped by their call sites. The header of godot/sim/js_compat.gd now records the numbers and the cost cuts to use
(cache 1 - exp(-k * dt) per constant for dt = FIXED_DT, no recomputation of known angles, a fused sin+cos, or JsMath in a GDExtension built with -ffp-contract=off as the design 12 option;
the 20-40 times figure for that option is an estimate, not a measurement). The scenario has to be measured as soon as the S5/S6 kernels exist, not at S9. Android speed is still unmeasured.
