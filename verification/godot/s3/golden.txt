Principle: no expected value is typed by hand. Pure declarations/functions are cut out of the original BY NAME, evaluated in node:vm with an EMPTY
sandbox (Math.random throws, DOM/Date/storage are ReferenceErrors) and written as deterministic JSON. --check regenerates in memory and
compares byte for byte; tools/run_godot_tests.sh calls it (through tools/check_generated.sh) right after tools/check_assets.sh.

4. Cross-check with a real Chromium (not part of --check: it needs Playwright)

tools/check_js_compat_consts.mjs (new, step 3/5 of tools/check_generated.sh): the 95 float constants of godot/sim/js_compat.gd, their CONST_BITS entries and the upstream literals of
  glibc usncs.h / s_sin.c / branred.h and V8 ieee754.cc (decimal literals together with the hex words quoted in the C comments, hex floats, high words) must agree bit for bit; SINCOSTAB_BITS (440)
  and TOVERP (75) must equal the copies in tools/golden/_glibc_sin.mjs (the transcription that tools/verify_golden_chromium.mjs checks against Chromium's Math.sin / Math.cos).
  Six mutations were each caught (EX_P3 consistently changed in expression and CONST_BITS, AT_T5 expression only, LG_5 missing from CONST_BITS, SINCOSTAB entry, TOVERP digit, LG_THIRD).

tools/census_rng_slots_chromium.mjs (new; needs Playwright + Chromium, so it is not part of check_generated.sh): runs the original in Chromium 141 with a gameRandom wrapper keyed by the
  running slot and compares the slots that draw with the consumes_rng_in_m1 marks of godot/sim/system_runner.gd. Output: rng_slots.txt. It exits 1 on a difference (checked with the old,
  wrong mark set: "check FAILED"). The output is deterministic (two runs identical).
